FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find answers to common questions regarding booking lead times, truck requirements, hygiene verification, and pricing structures.

For corporate lunches and private celebrations (50–300 guests), booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance is ideal to ensure your first-choice trucks. For high-demand dates, weddings, and large festivals (1,000+ guests), we recommend booking 2 to 6 months ahead. However, our emergency dispatch desk can frequently accommodate last-minute bookings within 48 to 72 hours.

Every mobile kitchen on the Food Feast platform is subjected to rigorous upfront vetting: active city health department permits (minimum 92/100 inspection grade), certified food safety manager credentials (ServSafe), commercial fire suppression compliance, and minimum $2,000,000 General Liability insurance naming your venue as additional insured.

Catering is typically structured as a guaranteed minimum food spend (e.g., $1,200 to $2,500 depending on day of week and truck tier) or as a flat per-person package ($16 to $32 per guest including entrée, side, and beverage). There are zero hidden booking fees, travel surcharges, or unexpected generator fees—all line items are itemized transparently in your single contract.

Yes, absolutely. During the menu curation phase, you can specify exact dietary ratios. We partner with dedicated plant-based trucks, certified Halal kitchens, and allergen-safe operators who maintain separate prep stations and dedicated fryers to prevent cross-contamination.

A standard food truck requires approximately 28 to 32 feet of level parking space with at least 12 feet of overhead clearance. Most of our partner trucks are fully self-sufficient with ultra-quiet commercial inverter generators. If your venue prefers shore power (e.g., 220V 50A or 110V 20A), we ensure exact cord adapters and power distribution boxes are deployed.

Food truck operators can submit an onboarding application in under 10 minutes via our Vendor Portal. Our compliance team verifies your health inspections, menu photos, and insurance certificates. Once approved, you gain access to high-ticket catering leads, automated calendar management, and direct deposit next-day payouts.

All contracts include weather contingency clauses. Trucks are equipped with weather awnings and order windows. For outdoor corporate events or weddings experiencing severe weather, we provide options to shift parking positioning closer to entryways, setup tented serving corridors, or reschedule without losing your deposit pursuant to our fair weather policy.

Our fleet operates on a strict 30-to-45-second per ticket throughput target. For 500 attendees with a 60-minute dining window, our concierges deploy a multi-truck fleet (typically 3 to 4 trucks with synchronized service lanes) or pre-boxed mobile buffet lines so queues never exceed 3 to 4 minutes per guest.
